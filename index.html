Hemodynamic Reference

Normal Aortic Pressure

Brisk systolic upstroke, dicrotic notch and diastolic runoff.

Damped Pressure

Reduced amplitude, sluggish upstroke and blunted waveform. Immediately notify the physician.

Ventricularized Pressure

Disproportionately reduced diastolic pressure with a ventricular-like contour. Possible coronary ostial obstruction. Alert the operator immediately. Injection should wait until the operator confirms safety.
AO 97/63 vs. peripheral BP 131/78

Immediately communicate the discrepancy.

"Your aortic pressure is damped compared with the peripheral pressure."

Compare waveform morphology, catheter position, pressure source, transducer level and zero with the team.
